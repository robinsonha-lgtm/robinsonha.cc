ABOUT

I'm Robinson, an interaction designer & creative technologist focused on research & strategy, systems, and service design.

At Recidiviz, I work on design systems and design resident facing tools that make bureaucratic processes legible and transparent, in order to empower the most vulnerable to act towards self determination and create agency for themselves.

As technology reshapes how we think and connect, I see it as a tool for positive change. Used with care and creativity, it can amplify collective effort in a world where globalization and digital systems are bringing us closer than ever.
